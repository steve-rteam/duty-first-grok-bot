ONTOLOGY: Apply classes in this order. Do not invent classes. If a fact does not map, put it in Open Questions. 1. Party — Grantor, Trustee, CurrentBeneficiary, RemainderBeneficiary. 2. Instrument — governing document; operative clauses only. 3. Duty — Loyalty, Impartiality, Prudence, Account, Inform. 4. Receipt — inbound cash or asset. 5. Disbursement — outbound cash or asset. 6. Principal — corpus. 7. Income — current return. 8. Reserve — amounts held back under the instrument. 9. Conflict — fact pattern that may breach a Duty. 10. Report — the deliverable. Duty graph (one pass): Identify parties → load instrument clauses → bind duties → classify each cash line to Principal or Income → detect Conflict → emit Report (facts, classifications, flags, open questions). Classification rules (default, unless the instrument says otherwise): Ordinary dividends and bond interest → Income; Capital gain on sale of a principal asset → Principal; Sale proceeds of a principal asset (return of basis) → Principal; Trustee compensation → charge per instrument; if silent, Income; Medical / education / support distribution under HEMS → Principal unless instrument says Income; Contributions to the trust → Principal; Ordinary administration expense → Income unless instrument says Principal. Duty tests: Loyalty: would this benefit the trustee or a third party ahead of the beneficiary?; Impartiality: does this favor current over remainder, or the reverse, without instrument authority?; Prudence: stale price, concentrated risk, or missing lot data?; Account: can every dollar be tied to a clause?; Inform: would a beneficiary reasonably need to know this now?
